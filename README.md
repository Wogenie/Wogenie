## 👋 Hello, I'm Wogenie

I'm a **Data Scientist and AI/ML Engineer** based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, passionate about designing, developing, and truly understanding intelligent systems. My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and AI engineering** — from cleaning and exploring messy data to training models, evaluating them honestly, and shipping them into real products.

I don't just want models that score well. I want to understand **why** they work, **when** they fail, and **how** they create real value for people. That curiosity is what pulls me toward neural network architectures, large language models, and applied AI that solves problems worth solving.

> *Building intelligent systems. Exploring model architectures. Solving real-world problems with data and AI.*

## 💡 How I Think About Data & AI

- **Questions before models.** A well-framed problem beats a fancy architecture every time.
- **Data quality is the real model.** Better data, careful features, and honest validation do the heavy lifting.
- **Reproducibility matters.** Clean pipelines, clear metrics, and results people can trust.
- **Impact over benchmarks.** A simple model that ships and helps people is worth more than a perfect one in a notebook.
- **Keep learning.** The field moves fast; curiosity and fundamentals keep you grounded.
